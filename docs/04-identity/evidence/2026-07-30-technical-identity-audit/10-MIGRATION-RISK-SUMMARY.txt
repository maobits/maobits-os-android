
=== MIGRATION RISK SUMMARY ===
This is an inventory phase only. No source file replacement was performed.

High-risk areas to plan before migration:
- applicationId: affects install/update continuity and app data.
- namespace: affects generated R/BuildConfig and package references.
- Kotlin package declarations/imports: affects compilation and refactoring scope.
- custom permissions: affects receiver/service access and manifest references.
- FileProvider authorities: affects URI grants and sharing.
- deep links/schemes: affects external intents and compatibility.
- debug/test references: may fail after migration if not updated intentionally.
- Wear module identity: must stay compatible with paired mobile app strategy.
