=== MAOBITS OS ANDROID - REAL DEVICE MESH DISCOVERY FINDING ===

Date:
2026-07-30

Finding:
Two smartphones installed Maobits OS and entered the default channel, but initially did not discover each other.

Root cause:
One or both devices had very low battery level.

Resolution:
After resolving the low battery condition, device discovery worked.

Technical interpretation:
Low battery conditions can restrict BLE scanning, BLE advertising, foreground services, background execution, and nearby-device discovery on Android devices.

Conclusion:
No code change required for this finding.

Testing rule added:
Real-device mesh discovery tests must be performed with battery level above 30%, battery saver disabled, Bluetooth enabled, location enabled, and app battery usage unrestricted.

Status:
Solved.
